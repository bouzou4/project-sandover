# Sandover knowledge base

- `architecture.md`: what the fork adds over OpenGOAL and where: subsystems, frame flow, settings flow, data pipeline.
- `glossary.md`: terms used across the notes.
- `decisions/`: architecture decision records.
- `facts/`: engine facts verified against source, with the pinned revision.
- `findings/`: dated investigation notes with measurements.
- `howto/`: how to build, run, capture and tune each feature.
- `symptoms.md`: observed defects with their mechanism and fix.
- `perf-backlog.md`: known costs left for the performance pass.
- Older findings name `$SANDOVER_DATA` folders that were later renamed to their topic in the docs (experiments/shadows, shots/stereo, experiments/fp16, audio, openxr, build/unbake-decomp, par-vista-*, haze-root, constshader); local folders may still carry the older names.
