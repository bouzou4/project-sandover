## Answer (≤5 lines)
4K60 with everything maxed is missed by a wide margin. Config A (FSR 3.1 at 100 %, i.e. native
TAA): 2 of 21 spots under 16.7 ms (finalboss 9.6, maincave-crystals 12.4), mean 33 ms, worst
village1-hut-lamps 80 ms. Config B (FSR 4 at 67 %): 9 of 21, mean 22 ms. Vanilla rendering (C)
costs 0.6-2.2 ms of GPU work per frame. The biggest costs are the PBR materials row (median
15.7 ms), the SSAO row (9.4 ms, far above its own 0.8 ms pass), the shadows row (7.0 ms), and
the `tie` renderer (8.8 ms mean, 28.6 ms max). FSR 4 blocks the render thread for most of the
frame in the interop hand-over.

## 1. Setup
- Machine class: a desktop with one RDNA 4 discrete GPU (32 GB; a second identical card sits
  idle, and gk runs on the GL context's adapter), a Zen 5 X3D 8-core CPU and a 4K 165 Hz HDR panel.
  This GPU has no earlier baseline: the 2026-10-10 profile ran on a different (NVIDIA) GPU, and
  the renderer has changed a lot since then (temporal AA only, motion vectors, motion blur,
  contact shadows, parallax self-shadow). Do not compare the two numbers directly.
- Build: master `dd8a20086` (gk + goalc, Release, `-j` = logical threads - 2 under the build
  lock), staged as `$SANDOVER_DATA/bin/perf-rdna4`. Project root staged per `rig-capture.md`
  ("Worktree builds") at `$SANDOVER_DATA/worktrees/perf-rdna4-root`, with master's `goal_src`
  compiled into its `out/` (see 6 for two gaps in that recipe).
- Tool: `sandover-perf` (`shots/perf.yaml`, 21 spots, 3840x2160, vsync and limiter off,
  `-nosound`), one instance, every timer window solo: no other gk, GPU 3D engine idle before
  each boot (checked by the wrapper), and for runs A2 and D2 a sampler logged per-process GPU
  3D utilisation and the foreground window every 10 s (`sampler.log`): only gk and DWM (under
  2 %) used the GPU.
- After-load forms (a wrapper around `sandover.rig.perf` replaces `ALL_ON`):
  - A: `sandover-settings-reset!`, then contact shadows on, motion blur strong (3), SSAO quality
    high, shadow quality high (the top tiers that exist on master), upscaler FSR 3.1 (0),
    render scale 100 %, `sandover-settings-reapply`. Parallax self-shadow runs at its strong
    default; the eco sprite sort is on (Sandover path). Camera motion blur stays at its default
    (Vehicles), so it is off at every spot.
  - B: A with upscaler FSR 4 (2) and render scale 67 % (2560x1440 into 4K).
  - C: `vanilla?` #t (upstream path, MSAA 2x from the manifest).
- Windows: 120 frames for the configuration runs, 60 frames for the per-feature deltas
  (A/B/A per toggle; the tool drops the first window after every switch). Delta runs reject
  windows with a frame over 100 ms (up to 4 retries) instead of the tool's 250 ms; see 5.
- Data: `$SANDOVER_DATA/perf/rdna4/` (`A2`, `B-r1`, `C-r1` configurations; `D2` deltas;
  `P` pan; `probe`; `A`, `A-r1`, `D` earlier runs, see 5).
- The engine timers log a mean, a minimum and a maximum per window, not percentiles, so the
  tables give the mean and the worst frame over the base windows instead of a p95.

## 5. FSR 4 interop stall (CPU against GPU frame time, run B-r1)

| spot | CPU frame | GPU frame | render thread | in the interop (`fsr4-sync-cpu-ms`) | `fsr4` GL span | FSR 4 on its queue | A `fsr3` |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| open-sea-high | 12.2 | 12.2 | 10.7 | 6.1 | 3.76 | 1.60 | 2.07 |
| jungle | 25.0 | 25.0 | 23.4 | 19.1 | 4.39 | 1.78 | 2.11 |
| darkcave | 35.6 | 36.1 | 26.9 | 25.4 | 14.64 | 2.09 | 2.26 |
| village1-hut-lamps | 47.2 | 47.1 | 43.7 | 40.0 | 6.43 | 1.74 | 4.57 |
| maincave-crystals | 9.6 | 9.5 | 8.1 | 7.0 | 3.71 | 1.83 | 1.61 |
| finalboss | 9.0 | 9.0 | 7.6 | 6.5 | 3.99 | 2.17 | 1.46 |

At every spot the render thread spends 70-90 % of the frame blocked in the WGL interop
hand-over (the unlock waits for GL's frame so far, the lock for the dispatch), so CPU frame =
GPU frame and the CPU never runs ahead of the GPU. The dispatch itself is 1.6-2.2 ms on the
D3D12 queue, but the GL span around it is 3.4-6.4 ms (14.6 at darkcave): 2-4.5 ms of GPU idle
per frame in the round trip, against `fsr3` at 1.4-4.6 ms. This matches the open item in
`2026-10-22-fsr4.md`; the GPU semaphore route there is the lever.

## 6. Ranked costs for the optimisation wave (no fixes proposed)
1. PBR materials row: median 15.7 ms (6.3-31.2), mostly in `tie` (8.8 ms mean, 28.6 max) and
   `tfrag` (4.0 mean, 8.6 max).
2. SSAO row: median 9.4 ms (4.5-24.1) against a 0.8 ms `ssao` pass: the cost sits in the
   geometry passes while the row is on (normal/emissive targets), largest in tie-heavy spots.
3. Shadows row: median 7.0 ms (3.5-13.3).
4. Neighbour levels (`vista`): median 4.4 ms (0-10.7), `vista` group up to 22 ms at
   open-sea-low in a slow boot.
5. Motion blur: 2.3 ms flat at 4K whatever the level (pass 2.5-2.9 ms), 0.6-0.9 ms at 67 %.
6. HDR present encode (`present-encode`): 2.75 ms mean, 5.5 max.
7. FSR 3.1: 2.5 ms mean at native (`fsr3-luma` 1.0 of it).
8. Tile lights (`emissive-lights`): 2.0 ms mean, 10.2 max (village1-hut-lamps).
9. FSR 4 interop round trip: 2-4.5 ms of GPU idle per frame and a serialised CPU (5).
10. Contact shadows 0.8 ms; parallax self-shadow up to 1.6 ms in tie-heavy night spots.

Anomalies to look at with the wave (measured, not explained):
- `actor-shadows` in config B reads 7.9-15.0 ms at sunken and the two lamp spots, against
  under 1 ms in A at the same spots.
- darkcave under FSR 4: `fsr4` 14.6 ms GL span (3.4-6.4 elsewhere).
- Run-to-run spread (2): boots where every pass ran 2-5x slower.
- Two of the first boots ended in a lost device: `hdr: Present failed (0x887a0005)` and
  `GL_CONTEXT_LOST` during the beach level load (configs A and B), with no driver reset in the
  system log; the same spots ran clean in four later boots.
- `glDrawBuffers` `GL_INVALID_OPERATION` (attachment `0x8ce0`) from the tfrag/tie buckets on the
  first frames after the motion vector targets are allocated.

## 7. Procedure notes
- `sandover-perf` never writes `out/` (its `Game` compiles for types only), so a staged root
  whose `goal_src` is newer than its `out/` runs the old compiled GOAL silently. The wrapper here
  booted with a writing `(mi)` when `outputs_fresh()` is false.
- The staged root also needs `third-party/fidelityfx/` (FSR 3.1's shader headers): without it
  gk logs `fsr3: unavailable (GL 4.3, headers missing), vanilla rendering in its place`.
- Both are now in `rig-capture.md` ("Worktree builds") and `perf.md` ("Another build").
