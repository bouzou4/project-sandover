## Answer (≤5 lines)
GTAO replaces SAO on every ambient occlusion tier (design decision 2026-10-19; the SAO kernel, its
blur and its presets are deleted). XeGTAO's main pass and edge-aware denoiser run as GLSL 4.10
fragment passes in the existing SSAO pipeline, with an AO history of their own (motion-vector
reprojection) and a stencil early-out for texels the composite discards. Offline on synthetic depth
GTAO creases are darker than SAO's at the same spread and low-poly facets do not shade; the live
A/B against the SAO deep look, the four-tier sheet and the convergence runs are pending.

Retune `intensity` per tier from that fit only (the response stays 0.5). Views 1-2 show `1 - occlusion^0.5`, so a script reads them as SAO views.

History on must settle below history off on static pixels, or the history is still rejecting. The grass and run cases are still open; static texels and smearing are proven (see "Live proof").

## Live proof (2026-10-22)
Build of `122213ffd` (review fixes), one rig instance, `--play-look` (FSR 3.1 at the default scale),
`beach-v1-noon` camera, AO view 2, 120 settle frames. Captures: `$SANDOVER_DATA/shots/gtao-417-proof/`
(`probe/`, `still-*` 3840x2160 four-frame still bursts, `pan/` 2560x1440 four-frame pans at 0.75 deg
per frame). History off is `(pc-set-ssao-param 24 0)` on the same build.
- Symptom: per-frame noise on the static world (play report, 2026-10-21, "kind of noisy").
- Mechanism: the history's expected clip w was w_prev / w_cur against a stored w in game units, so the
  5 % test rejected every static texel and the frame showed raw per-frame noise after denoise.
- Proof, still camera: frame-to-frame RMS (8-bit AO view) over flat shaded pixels (mean under 240,
  5x5 gradient under 1.5 per pixel; 8.1 % of the frame at medium, 6.6 % at ultra):

| Tier | history off | history on | pixels changing by more than 2 (box on the tower) |
|---|---|---|---|
| medium | 7.21 | 1.43 | 29.0 % -> 2.0 % |
| ultra | 5.02 | 1.38 | 24.2 % -> 10.7 % |

  With the history on, the frame-to-frame difference image is black on flat surfaces and keeps only
  edges and foliage; with it off the whole shaded world flickers. What remains at ultra is an
  edge-shaped (embossed) difference that the history-off burst shows too, i.e. the projection jitter
  moving full-resolution silhouettes, not rejected texels.
- Proof, moving camera (no smearing): pan frame k with the history against the history-off frame of
  the same camera k and of the previous camera k-1 (where a lagging history would sit), mean absolute
  difference over k = 1..3: 2.5-2.7 against k, 11.6-14.3 against k-1; on the 5 % strongest edges 11.0
  against k, 34.7-47.9 against k-1 (the history-off step between k-1 and k is 36-51). No trail in crops.
- The rig logs `glDrawBuffers` invalid-operation errors for a few frames while the targets are
  allocated, on base builds too; not from this branch.

## Strength and noise (2026-10-21)
Play test, live 4K on the integration build (village2, ultra, FSR 3.1 at 67 %): "way too strong,
significantly stronger than the SSAO we had, almost pitch-black shadows, and kind of noisy"; Ultra
reads strong.
- Mechanism (strength): XeGTAO's response, `visibility^2.2` with a 0.03 floor, composited at strength
  1.0, against SAO deep's `(1 - sqrt(occlusion))^0.9` at strength 0.9. GTAO's darkening is convex in
  occlusion and reaches 0.97; SAO's is `sqrt`-shaped and tops out near 0.6. The 2026-10-19 tuning
  matched mean and p95 only, where the two curves cross. On the eight `master-ref` cameras (AO views,
  white normalised to 249) the medium candidate had the same mean but p99 +24..+60 % and p99.9
  0.62-0.85 against SAO's 0.43-0.60; 0.4-0.6 % of the pixels were darker than 0.7 where SAO had none.
  Pooled quantiles: SAO darkening = 0.70 x sqrt(GTAO occlusion) within 0.68-0.75 from p80 to p99.99
  (`occlusion = 1 - v^(1/2.2)`). Ruled out: the composite target (unchanged from SAO: the full lit
  colour, both); the history (averaging the convex shaped value biases it lighter, not darker); the
  half-res upsample (ultra is full resolution and the reporter saw it there). Thin occluder compensation
  0.5 needs a higher intensity for the same match and fits worse (10.5 % against 9.7 %).
- All strength numbers in this section are offline or from stills, and none used the history: the
  history rejected every static texel until the review fixes (below), so the fit describes the
  kernel, response and denoise alone. Step 2 re-checks it with the history running.
- Fit: response 0.5 on occlusion, applied in the composite after the history and denoise; one
  intensity for all cameras: radius 1.25, intensity 0.70, mean |error| over mean/p95/p99/p99.9
  15 / 12 / 6 / 6 % (the old shaping at its best intensity: 36 / 34 / 15 / 24 %).
- Tiers: low, medium, high intensity 0.70, ultra 0.67 (ultra's old stills sat about 5 % above high
  in mean and p99); all tiers radius 1.25, thin 0; the cap `max_darkening` 0.8 applies to the
  character weight too (it used to clamp at 1). Slices, steps and resolution carry the difference.
- Noise source (stills, high-pass std at 1-3 px over shaded pixels): SAO deep 0.025-0.037, GTAO
  medium 0.044-0.056, old-design high 0.049-0.059, ultra 0.069-0.070, i.e. 1.5-2.2x SAO and worst on
  ultra (a 3x3 denoise covers half the screen footprint at full resolution). Hypothesis, not yet
  backed by a live run: the AO history reprojected the jittered depth with the unjittered camera
  matrix and ignored the history's own jitter, so each frame misregistered by up to one render
  pixel and edges (grass, rock silhouettes) failed the clip-w test. The review found a larger cause
  that hides this one: the expected clip w was in the wrong units, so no static texel accumulated
  anywhere and the noise in these stills is the raw per-frame noise after denoise, edges or not.
  Changes made: history reprojection removes this frame's jitter and adds the history's
  (`scene_jitter`), two denoise passes after the history (XeGTAO's medium setting) instead of one,
  and a joint bilateral upsample for the half-res tiers instead of the nearest-depth pick (2x2
  blocks that flipped with the jitter). Which of them matters is step 4's question.
- Profiler: "finish() not called on ssao" (and on "[56] common-pris-generic") came from
  `ProfilerNode` children in a `std::vector`: a sibling added while a scoped child was open
  (contact shadows or swamp mist after ssao; ssao while the bucket 56 node was open) reallocated it
  and the open node finished freed memory. Children are a `std::list` now. It was not specific to the
  history-off path.

## Design
- Tiers (the preset index is the `ssao-quality` value; schema 4):

| Tier | Preset | Slices x steps | AO resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| low | 0 `deep-low` | 1 x 2 (XeGTAO low) | half |
| medium (default) | 1 `deep` | 2 x 2 (XeGTAO medium) | half |
| high | 2 `deep-high` | 2 x 3 | half |
| ultra | 3 `deep-ultra` | 3 x 3 (XeGTAO high) | full |

  All share deep's radius; intensity 0.70 (ultra 0.67), see Strength and noise. The high/ultra split
  is from pass counts and the offline comparison; the combined perf pass may move it. A version 3
  `sandover.gc` reads `high` (SAO deep) as medium and `medium`, `low` as low. Every tier runs as
  saved outside the temporal AA path too (stereo, XR, MSAA): the renderer has no tier limit there and
  runs the fixed pattern with three denoise passes (the old clamp in `sandover-effective!` is gone).
- Kernel (`ssao_ao.frag`): faithful to `XeGTAO.hlsli` (Intel, MIT, licence in the shader): slices
  with Hilbert + R2 noise, R1 step jitter, distribution power 2, mip `log2(offset) - 3.3`, falloff
  over the last 0.615 of `1.457 x radius`, the thickness heuristic (`gtao_thin`), the `iarc`
  integral with the 0.05 projected-length fudge, no final power or floor here (the composite applies `intensity x occlusion^0.5`). Deviations: GL y-up
  pixels (omega keeps sin's sign); each slice's horizons start at the depth facet's plane and
  visibility is relative to that facet's own integral (a flat unoccluded facet is exactly 1: the
  GTAO form of SAO's two-plane test against low-poly faceting); SAO's same-smooth-surface sample
  rule kept; sqrt for the fast-sqrt trick; no bent normals; float target, no packing scale.
- Exclusion: an R8 mask from a stencil-tested draw against the render target's depth-stencil
  texture (bit 7 = 1, bit 6 characters = 0.5). Excluded texels (any sample) skip the kernel and are
  stored negative; the history and the denoiser skip them; the composite's upsample ignores them
  and its stencil gate is unchanged. Without a depth-stencil texture no mask: every texel runs.
- History (`TemporalAccum`, a shared helper for any per-frame-noisy scalar, e.g. a dithered contact
  shadow): RG16F (value, clip w) ping-pong at the AO resolution; reprojects with
  `motion_vectors::frame().cam_reproj` from the render depth, or with the merc object velocity
  (`FrameState::obj_tex`, new) where the character surface is the visible one; over history texels
  whose clip w matches the expected one (5 %, 10 % on object velocity): bilinear at full resolution,
  at half resolution a squared radial tent (radius 1.6 px) around each texel's kept pixel; blend 0.9
  static, 0.6 on characters (object velocity or stencil bit 6); restarts on a motion-vector cut, a
  resize or a frame without motion vectors. Then two XeGTAO denoise passes. The reprojection removes the projection jitter of both frames. Cost: one pass reading the
  signal, depth (all samples), object velocity and four (full res) or nine (half res) history
  texels, writing one RG16F texel.
  `(pc-set-ssao-param 24 0)` turns it off for the A/B.
- Without motion vectors (stereo, XR, upstream rendering): a fixed noise pattern and three denoise
  passes, logged once.
- Denoise (`ssao_denoise.frag`): XeGTAO's 3x3 edge-aware pass (symmetric edges, leak, diagonals,
  beta 1.2 final and 0.24 otherwise), edges from the z mip of its resolution, unquantised.
- Engine: SsaoParams radius, intensity, merc weight, luma floor, cave scale, gtao_slices, steps,
  full_res, thin (`pc-set-ssao-param` 1, 2, 6-8, 19-22); 23 denoise passes, 24 history (dev);
  XeGTAO constants and history blends in the ImGui SSAO window.
- Version: fragment shaders `#version 410 core`, no compute.

## Review fixes (2026-10-21)
Code read only; no launch (the GPU was reserved).
- Expected clip w: `cam_reproj` maps clip to clip and the shader feeds it `(ndc, nz, 1)`, i.e. this
  frame's clip divided by its w, so `h.w` is w_prev / w_cur (about 1) while the stored history holds
  clip w in game units. The 5 % test rejected every static texel on the camera path; only merc
  texels with object velocity accumulated. Now `expect_w = h.w * w`. Still camera: `cam_reproj` is
  the identity, `h.w = 1`, so `expect_w = w`, the value the history stored for that texel with the
  same `wk` (the jitter moves the sample, not the camera).
- Half-res history: texel t holds full-res pixel `2t + (t.y & 1, t.x & 1)`, not the block centre,
  so a regular-grid bilinear landed 0.25 texel off on a still camera and mixed neighbours every
  frame. Each candidate is now weighted by `(1 - d / 1.6)^2` around its kept pixel; 1.6 px covers
  the widest gap between kept pixels (1.58 px) and leaves 1.3 % to neighbours on a still camera
  (bilinear on the regular grid: 44 %). Acceptance compares the matching weight with the total.
- `pc-set-ssao-preset` with an index past the table logs an error and leaves AO as it was (it used
  to turn AO off); negative still turns it off. Shot manifests and runbooks that sent the old deep
  index 4 now send 1 (medium, tuned to read as deep).

## Second review (2026-10-22)
- History w units: the history (RG16F) and the object velocity target (RGBA16F) stored clip w raw,
  i.e. view depth in game units times the projection's `persp[2][3]`; both now hold w / 4096 and every
  reader compares in those units. Not a reproduced defect: at `beach-v1-noon` (depth from AO view 4)
  flat shaded pixels at 20-200 m accumulated before the change, frame-to-frame RMS history on / off
  0.83 / 7.26 (medium), 0.80 / 5.19 (ultra); after it 0.80 (medium) and 1.01 (ultra), on four-frame
  4K bursts scored from frames 1-3 (`$SANDOVER_DATA/shots/gtao-417-proof/after-wmetres2/`, `depth/`).
  The change is headroom for the half-float range, not a measured fix.
- NaN: `fastACos` clamps its input to 1 and the history treats a NaN signal as unoccluded and skips
  NaN history texels. Hardening only: no NaN was observed.
- Effects drop (AO and tile lights dying until the vanilla switch): proven elsewhere and not a NaN.
  The play launcher sent `(pc-set-ssao-preset 4)` after every warp; with four GTAO presets the old
  `pc_set_ssao_preset` turned AO off for an index it could not apply. `122213ffd` (an unknown index logs
  and leaves AO alone) is the fix. `scripts/effects_after_resize.py` fails on the old play build at the
  old launcher's AO probe (white AO share 0.853 -> 0.177) and passes all 16 probes on the play base plus
  the fix. Details: `kb/findings/2026-10-22-effects-drop-after-resize.md`.
- Settings: the clamp that ran a saved high or ultra as medium outside the temporal AA path is gone
  (a setting does only what its row says). The renderer runs every tier without motion vectors,
  stereo and XR included (per eye, fixed pattern, three denoise passes).
