## Answer (≤5 lines)
Partly. At strength 1 the class detail reads on sunlit ground (sand, dirt paths, grass) as a fine
painterly grain and the soft banded highlight is barely visible; at 2 the stone grain turns into
speckle noise. Most of the frame moves little because the sidecar keeps shading to sun-dominated,
unshadowed surfaces, and noon sun is nearly overhead on the tested levels. Where the sun share is ~0
a weaker key light from the mood table takes over. Metal and precursor barely show: the big precursor
pieces tested (village1 oracle, jungle tower walls, finalboss ledge) are drawn by TIE geometry that
reaches the screen outside the shaded path. Toggle off passes the parity gate (56/56).

## Per class
- **stone** (sand, dirt, rock): reads best. The coarse grain is right at 1.0 on sand and paths;
  vertical cliffs change little at noon (sun overhead, low share). At 2.0 it is noisy.
- **foliage**: grass cards and fronds pick up a soft sheen; veins are too fine to see at game distance.
- **wood**: hard to see; fences and huts are mostly in the bake's shade or face away from the sun.
- **metal / precursor**: no convincing showing. In the jungle tower the metal floor disc and door
  get the key light (visibility view shows it), but at noon the key is near vertical and the camera
  sees the disc at a grazing angle, so no highlight lands; the carved walls around it are not on a
  shaded path. The village1 oracle (outdoor, in sun, precursor) is unchanged for the same reason;
  only the cliffs around it pick up stone detail.
- **organic (merc)**: key-light highlight works after flipping the camera-space view vector
  (`pbr_merc_vsign = -1`, checked with the front-facing view); effect on Jak is a thin rim, subtle.
- **glass, cloth, snow, lava**: no rows in the current CSV (0 textures), untested.
- **special**: untouched, as intended.

## Key light where there is no sun
Where the sidecar's sun share is ~0 (interiors, deep shade, levels whose slots have no fitted sun),
the highlight and detail use the mood-table direction per palette slot (`dir_table`, what
`update-mood-palette` copies into light-group 0 for characters), blended by the live palette
weights, coloured by the fitted slot light and weighted by `(1 - sun share) × a × ambient`
× `pbr_key` (0.5 by default). Draws without sidecar records fall back to a baked-luminance proxy.
Levels with no sidecar at all still get nothing (the table direction lives in the sidecar header);
feeding light-group 0 from the game would need a `TfragPcPortData` field.

## Envmapped TIE and generic coverage
Revision 1 left the finalboss sand ledge, the jungle tower walls and the village1 oracle at their
vanilla colour in the class view. Masking renderer categories from GOAL (`*vu1-enable-user-menu*`)
at finalboss-start: clearing `tie`/`tie-near` removes the ledge; clearing `tfrag`/`trans-tfrag`,
`generic` or the shrub masks does not. The class shading now also runs in:

- **envmapped TIE, base pass** (`etie_base`): same background include as tfrag (sidecar records
  where the tree has them, triplanar detail, sun and key highlight). The envmap second pass
  (`etie`) is untouched, so the reflection stays vanilla and the highlight is added under it.
- **generic** (`Generic2`, non-full-matrix draws): class per draw from the pool texture's
  combo_id (tbp lookup, not for mt4hh), face normal and UV detail in camera space, sun and key
  directions rotated into camera space from the last background setup. The generic vertex format
  (`Generic2::Vertex`) carries no normals, only position, colour, UV and flags, so the normal is
  the derivative face normal. The light frame (`rot^T`, taken from `make_new_cam_mat`) is inferred,
  not checked on a generic draw.

Which path each object takes (class view): in revision 1, with only the plain TIE path shaded, all
three kept their vanilla colour while merc and tfrag were coloured. With the ETIE base pass and
generic shaded they show their class colour (metal blue; the finalboss ledge is metal too), and
they keep it with the `generic` mask cleared. So the ledge, the tower walls and the oracle are
**envmapped TIE, drawn by Tie3's ETIE base pass**, not generic. None of the tested spots exercises
the generic path.

What shows after the change (recaptures `shots/pbr/{off,s05,s10,s20,classes,vis}/` for
jungle-tower and village1-oracle): the oracle is precursor (human ruling, 2026-09-25): its eight village1 textures
(`precursor-metal-*`, `precursor-tube-script-01`, `gen-precursor-tube-script`,
`vil1-precursor-*`) were judged plain `metal` with no tag and are now corrected in
`materials.csv` (`source=human`, tag `precursor`); recaptured, it shows the bronze class in the
class view, a faint bronze highlight on the crown at strength 2 and is near vanilla at 1; the tower walls change very little. ETIE draws without
sidecar records fall back to the baked-luminance proxy, which in the tower treats the lit interior
walls as sunlit (visibility view: red), so the key light never takes over there. That is a proxy
limit, not a path gap; the per-texture maps step should drive interiors from the key light when a
draw has no records and the level's slot has no fitted sun.

## Classifier gap: precursor tag
321 rows have `precursor` in the name; only 6 carried the tag before the oracle correction (now
14). 261 are `metal` without the tag, so they shade as plain metal, not bronze. The name is a
reliable signal here (glow, bluelight and glass variants are rightly `special`), so a rule
(`metal` + name contains `precursor` -> tag) or a re-judge pass with that guidance would close it.

## Shader inputs for per-texture maps (next step)
The procedural detail array is a placeholder; per-texture normal, roughness and metallic maps
keyed by combo_id (`sandover-materials derive-maps`) replace it. What the engine needs to plug
them in, per shaded path:

- **Samplers.** A second texture per draw next to the albedo: normal (RG, tangent space, glTF
  +Y up) on one unit and roughness on another (or packed as B/A of the normal texture). Bound
  alongside `tex_T0` wherever the albedo is bound: tfrag/tie/ETIE base (`tree_tex_id`), shrub,
  merc (`draw.texture`), generic (pool lookup by tbp). The loader builds them next to the level
  textures, indexed the same way; a missing map binds a flat 1x1 (normal 0.5,0.5; roughness 1).
  Metallic is a scalar per texture: a per-draw uniform like `pbr_mat`, or kept per class.
  Anim-slot and eye textures (negative / eye ids) get the flat default.
- **Tangent frame.** Maps are in texture space, so every path uses the UV cotangent frame from
  screen derivatives (as merc and generic do now), with handedness from the sign of the UV
  Jacobian for mirrored islands. Backgrounds drop the triplanar world projection: UVs come from
  `tex_coord`, the geometric normal from the sidecar smooth normal where defined, else the face
  normal. No vertex format change.
- **Where the class uniform still applies.** Shading on/off (`special`, `lava`, `none`),
  highlight tint and albedo-tint mix (bronze for precursor), band shape, strength scale, the
  metallic default when a texture has no map, and the debug class view. Roughness from the map
  replaces the class `gloss` (gloss = f(roughness)); the class `detail` becomes a normal-map
  strength.
- **Unchanged inputs.** Sun share, visibility and the emissive mask from the sidecar; the key
  light where the sun share is ~0; merc key light; the parity rule (toggle off takes the vanilla
  path, no extra samples).

## What the fur shell shader needs
- The `fur` tag reaches the engine (bit 6 of the class byte) but only one texture carries it; the
  classifier must tag Daxter, lurkers and the rest before a shell pass can select them.
- Merc has no tangents; the shells can use the same derivative frame, but shell offset needs the
  skinned normal in the vertex shader (already there as `rotated_nrm`) and the vertex tint.
- The key-light share (`pbr_key.w`) is a usable rim/sheen driver for fur tips.

## Limits
- Shaded: tfrag, tie (plain and envmapped base pass), shrub, merc and non-full-matrix generic.
  Not shaded: wind tie, emerc, full-matrix generic and sprites.
- Levels without an `.unbake` sidecar get no background detail or highlight (no sun or key direction).
- Detail normals are procedural placeholders; Toksvig and derivative fade are not done.
- Cost was not measured with GPU timers in this spike; until the toggle is first
  turned on the off path makes no extra GL calls; after that it resets `pbr_on` once per tree.
