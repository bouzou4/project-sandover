## Answer (≤5 lines)
The tile-light pass now takes a second light list: the nearest 16 census emitters that are on,
from a table compiled into gk (`WorldLights.inc`, `sandover-lights bake`, 3,042 lights over 21
levels, 13-254 per level). Entity state comes from GOAL, which answers up to 64 actor-id queries
per frame from the display loop; hour masks and static E weights are resolved in the engine. An
in-view world light that a selected tile already stands for is dropped; arcs get a fixed power.

## State: GOAL publishes, the engine asks
GOAL publishing is the simpler of the two. The C++ side could read `entity-perm` status from
GOAL memory (fixed offsets), but that answers only "collected / complete". The gates the census
found need process state: a yellow eco sits in `die` until the player's eco runs out
(collectables.gc:530), a blocked vent is in `vent-blocked` (collectables.gc:1715), a crate's
contents are out only while it is in `special-contents-die` (crates.gc:709), a cave crystal
glows only in `cavecrystal-active` (darkcave-obs.gc:80). GOAL has all of that behind
`(-> links process state name)`. The engine lists the nearest 64 GOAL-gated lights each frame;
`pc-world-lights-update` (main.gc, after `update *pc-settings*`) looks each up with
entity-by-aid's binary search and returns 0..1000. Answers persist per light; never asked = off.
Particle hour masks use the hour GOAL passes. A static uses the weight the tfrag/tie draws give
its own E source this frame (`unbake_last_emissive_weights`), so a night lamp lights exactly when
its texture glows. Positions never need publishing: census emitters sit at their entity position.

## Shot selection (revision 2)
`sandover-lights spots` ranks every strong or lamp light whose gate is open at the shot hour by
added light over baked light on the geometry within its radius, and places a camera 3-5 m from the
lit patch with the emitter just outside the frame. Top of the ranking: the darkcave crystals
(added 0.05-0.10 over baked 0.07-0.15), then village1 lamps at night (0.10-0.16 over 0.44-0.56),
a snow bumper, citadel generators, beach and training lamps. Picked, best per kind on distinct
levels: darkcave `cavecrystal-5`, village1 lamp `static:21`, snow `snow-bumper-71`, plus the
temple top.

## Census correction
`:hour-mask` bits are the hours a part is skipped (sparticle-launcher.gc:746: `logtest? hour-bit
mask` -> skip), not the hours it runs. The census text now says "off at hours"; the bake reads
the list as off-hours either way. Village butterflies and moths run in daylight, not at night.

## Cost (estimate, not measured)
CPU: distance and state for the in-use levels' rows (at most ~700 with three levels loaded) and
two partial sorts, under 0.05 ms; GOAL: at most 64 binary searches and 129 kernel calls per frame.
GPU: one 32-texel pass, and the light pass loops over up to 16 more lights per opaque pixel
(the tile pass dominates the recorded 1.6 ms). The tile-lights debug window shows the CPU time;
GPU cost is the existing `emissive-lights-total` timer.

## Defaults and open points
Class intensities (strong 0.08, lamp 0.12, weak 0.01, arc 0.005, particle 0.05) are set from the
tile power scale and one capture round, not tuned: a vent entering the frame switches from its
world light to its tiles and may step in brightness. Hut lamps stay out (`maybe`); the village
lamp light comes from the `vil-lamp` statics. The crystal is `strong` (0.08); if revision 2 still
reads subtle, crystals are the first candidate for their own intensity.
