## Answer (≤5 lines)
Yes. SAO (G3D port) runs after bucket 56 on a second MSAA depth copy: linearize (farthest sample), z-mips, AO at half resolution, a separable bilateral blur, and a stencil-masked multiply over opaque pixels, with characters weighted separately. Cost at 2560×1440, MSAA 4, solo: **0.55-0.66 ms** for the whole chain across the five round candidates and three spots. That is inside the 1.0 ms budget, but the worst frame reaches about 2× the average. With SSAO off, captures match master within the rig's own run-to-run noise (village1-hut: 5 px differ by 1 level). The off path makes no GL calls (by construction, not traced). Temporal accumulation is not built; there is a hook for it. Stereo is per-eye by construction but could not be captured live because of an existing crash in the stereo rig.

## Evidence (path:line@sha / URL, short excerpts)
**Pipeline** (verified by code; `game/graphics/opengl_renderer/SsaoRenderer.cpp`, shaders `ssao_*.frag`)

| Pass | What | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| depth-copy | MSAA→MSAA depth blit after `GENERIC_PRIS` (56) | Its own texture; the SSAO bucket-30 capture is untouched |
| linearize | R32F linear z in metres, farthest (min raw) sample per pixel | `make_new_cam_mat` mapping; cleared depth → far plane (10 km) → ao = 1 |
| minify | z mips 1-4, rotated-grid subsample (SAO minify) | Samples only level i-1 (base/max level) while writing level i |
| ao-raw | SAO, half res, 12-16 spiral taps | Best-pair 4-tap normals from full-res z; SAO fall-off unchanged, in metres. `frame_spin` uniform = 0 is the temporal hook |
| temporal | temporal | not implemented |
| ao-blur | 2 × 1D bilateral blur, half res | Depth edge stop from SAO's key scale, made relative to the radius |
| composite | nearest-depth 4-tap upsample, `GL_DST_COLOR × src`, RGB only | Two stencil-tested draws: `(s & 0xC0) == 0` background at `intensity`, `== 0x40` characters at `intensity × merc_weight` |

**Stencil convention** (verified by code; `SsaoRenderer::set_bucket_stencil`, applied before each bucket only when SSAO is on). Buckets 0-4 set 0x80 (exclude). 5-44 clear 0x80. 45-46 (translucent merc/generic: effects, see-through actors) set 0x80. 47 (shadow) runs its own state with read/write mask 0x3F (`ShadowRenderer.cpp`, only when `ssao_stencil`). 48-56 write 0x40 with mask 0xC0: characters receive AO and are marked as dynamic for later temporal work. From 57 on, the stencil state is back to vanilla. Debug view 5 shows the mask: magenta for 0x80, green for 0x40. On the beach shot, sky and far ocean are magenta and Jak is green (`$SANDOVER_DATA/shots/ssao/dev/views.png`).

**Ground contact (revision 2; measured on captures).** The first version excluded all of buckets 31-46. On village1-hut that excluded the path Jak stands on, because the path is a blended tfrag layer over the opaque ground (stencil view, `dev/v1-stencil.png`). The ground under his feet therefore got no AO even though his depth was in the copy. Blended tfrag (trans, dirt, ice) now receives AO. These layers either lie on opaque ground and leave its depth, or write their own depth where alpha ≥ 0x7e. Only 45-46 stay excluded. AO still reads only the post-56 depth, which contains the characters. The bucket-30 copy is not used by SSAO at all; it remains only as the SSAO debug capture. After the change, Jak's feet darken the path around them: see `$SANDOVER_DATA/shots/ssao/village1-hut-contact-before-after.png`, colour crops over a ×10 difference against vanilla. Halo guard: the blur and the upsample are both depth-keyed, and translucent actors remain stencil-excluded. On blended tfrag pixels with alpha < 0x7e, AO follows the surface behind, which is mostly what shows through. Alpha-tested foliage (shrubs, 19-30) gets AO on the blades and contact darkening at the base of each tuft (`dev/foliage-check.png`). Thin blades also darken the ground just behind them in screen space. That is the usual SSAO thin-occluder effect, visible in `deep`.

All differing pixels sit on Jak's idle animation, his shadow edge, birds or butterflies. These are the known capture-rig nondeterminism sources (bounding boxes in `parity/pxdiff.py` output). Background, sky and ocean are identical. The inert pair's larger beach count is Jak and his shadow outline (`parity/diff-beach-start-noon.png`). No change shows on excluded pixels. Off path: `dispatch_buckets_jak1` reads `ssao_enable` once; every SSAO call (`set_bucket_stencil`, `render`, `reset_stencil`, `draw_view`) sits behind `if (ssao)`. `ShadowRenderer` uses mask 0xFF unless `ssao_stencil`, so when SSAO is off the frame issues exactly the vanilla GL call sequence. This is established by construction, not by an API trace.

Revision 2 (ground contact), second of two solo runs (`cost-1440p-msaa4-v2b.json`): the undisturbed windows show totals of 0.546-0.659 ms, with the same per-pass split. There is no measurable change, which is expected: the passes are the same and only the stencil mask differs. Disturbed windows appear in both runs (for example village1-hut deep at 1.23 ms, with a 0.40 ms copy). Numbers are recorded as measured. No optimization performed; it is deferred to the end of the project.

Worst single frame of the total: 0.61-1.21 ms, so up to 2.0× the average. That sits at the worst-frame limit (2x the pass average). The spikes come from the copy, like SSAO's. Run-to-run variance is large. The first full run gave about 2× these numbers everywhere (1.1-1.6 ms total, copy 0.40 ms), and a second run had isolated 1.0 ms windows. Both happened with no other gk running. The likely cause is the GPU's power state or other desktop GPU load; it was not isolated. Whole-dispatch "buckets" grows by 0.6-0.7 ms with SSAO on, which is a bit more than the sum of the passes (CPU bubbles; SSAO saw the same).

**Round candidates, FLIP vs off** (measured; 16-shot `auto-v0`, revision 2): faithful 0.0068 mean / 0.020 worst, grounded 0.028 / 0.072, contact 0.029 / 0.077, soft 0.042 / 0.107, deep 0.076 / 0.181. The spread is visible and ordered, and the faithful placeholder is the lowest (B2/B3). Sets are in `$SANDOVER_DATA/shots/ssao/<candidate>/`. Four labelled screening grids (`sandover-grid --captions ssao`, off + five candidates per row, with a one-line caption per candidate taken from the TOML's `caption`) are in `$SANDOVER_DATA/shots/ssao/sheets/`.

**Dark Cave floor** (measured; `darkcave-start`, deep): on pixels with vanilla luminance < 4, the mean change is -0.01 levels with the floor and +0.02 without it, and 0.04% of those pixels darken by more than one level. Lit pixels darken 0.71 levels on average (cave_scale 0.5) against 1.34 levels at scale 1. The floor needs a colour resolve blit, so it only runs while a fixed-slot level (darkcave, maincave, robocave, jungleb, lavatube, citadel) is loaded, detected from the loader's in-use level names.

**Stereo (inferred from code, not captured live).** Capture, AO, blur and composite all run inside each eye's `dispatch_buckets`, so each eye uses its own depth. The textures are reused one after the other and no state carries across eyes. The timers run twice per frame in stereo and report per-eye windows. A live stereo capture failed because enabling stereo before the rig's level load hits `Assertion failed: 'info.wind_idx * 4 <= m_wind_vectors.size()'` (Tie3 wind). The same crash happens with SSAO off, so it predates this work.

## Implications for the design
- The planned structure holds at about 0.6 ms, with room for temporal (estimated 0.04 ms) inside the 1.0 ms budget. The depth-copy stage is a third of the cost and causes the worst-frame spikes.
- Faceting on low-poly rocks and faint crease lines along sand triangle edges are visible in the AO views. Both are expected from depth-derived normals (v1). The v2 normal target removes them, and `bias` is the lever until then.
- Characters now cast AO onto the ground at the background strength. `merc_weight` scales only the AO on character pixels themselves.
- Presets are comparison candidates for the first SSAO choice, not a shipped feature.
