## Answer (≤5 lines)
Fur shells are drawn per texture: Merc2 re-issues a merc draw as instanced shells only when its
texture's class byte has the `fur` bit, and that bit comes from the `tags` column of
`materials.csv`, matched by `combo_id`. The tags were hand-set row by row: name families with
"fur" in them plus a few reviewer confirmations. The muse's coat textures are named by colour
(`muse-white`, `muse-pattern`, `muse-gold2brown`, `muse-stripe*`), so the first pass missed them, and
the one reviewer confirmation (`muse-ear2`) landed on the beach copy only. The fix makes `fur`
a name rule applied to every tpage copy.

## Mechanism
- `Loader` calls `pbr_classify_level` per level; it looks up each level texture's `combo_id`
  (`tpage << 16 | index`) in `materials.csv` and sets bit 6 when `tags` contains `fur`
  (`Materials.cpp`). Merc2 draws the shells after the base draw when
  `pbr_texture_fur(&lev->tex_class, draw.texture)` holds, so a texture without the bit gets
  the base draw only.
- The muse in the sculptor's hut is `*sculptor-muse-sg*` (`levels/beach/sculptor.gc`), art
  `sculptor-muse-ag` in `BEA.DGO`. The sculptor spawns it with `manipy-spawn` once the
  `misty-muse` task is past `need-reward-speech`. Its textures live in `beach-vis-pris`; the
  Misty Island chase uses `muse-ag` with the same names in `misty-vis-pris`.
- Of the muse's 1610 triangles, `muse-white` covers 678, `muse-gold2brown` 312 and
  `muse-pattern` 264; none of them was tagged. Only `muse-ear2` (60 triangles, the yellow ear
  rim) was, so at the bundle camera one muse draw per frame got shells: the ear rims.
- Tags are per row, and a row is one tpage copy. The reviewer confirmations of `muse-ear2`,
  `drill-furlight`, `evilbro-beard` and `flut-creamfeathers` each reached one copy, and a
  `merge` clears the tags of every unreviewed row, so the name families from the first pass
  were one re-merge away from disappearing.

## Fix
`[tag_rules.fur]` in `sandover/presets/materials.toml` lists texture-name globs;
`sandover-materials tag-rules` sets `fur` on every matching row in every tpage and clears it
on every other row, and `merge` re-applies the rule. The globs keep every name tagged before
except `ogre-nose` and add the muse coat and the furry creatures below. Criterion: the
texture paints the hair coat of a furry creature (body, limbs, head pelt, fur-to-skin
transitions), not eyes, lids, inner ears, mouths, noses, claws, bare skin, straps or clothing.
Against the 2026-10-09 table: 185 rows tagged, 102 added, 1 removed (`ogre-nose`, ogre
boss: a nose is outside the criterion, so the old hand tag goes), no other column touched.

## Needs a visual check in play
- `muse-goldsmall`: a flat yellow swatch classed `metal` on the Misty copy (`organic` on the
  beach copy); tagged with the coat because `muse-ear2`, the same flat yellow, was confirmed.
- `rammer-peltface`, `rammer-peltear`, `yeti-peltface`, `yeti-peltear`, `yeti-peltpaw`,
  `yeti-peltpaw2`: pelt textures that also carry a nose, an ear or paw pads.

## Limits
- The rule is a list of names, so a texture added to an actor later needs its glob.
- Shell cost scales with covered pixels; snow (yeti, ram boss, bunnies) and the yakows in
  Sandover gain shells and were not timed here.
