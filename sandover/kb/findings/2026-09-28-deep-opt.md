# Deep optimization run (2026-09-28): results

Integration branch `perf/deep-001` (master + the crystal lights + the perf tooling). Rule: a
concession invisible at 4K in normal play with every feature on is allowed; a visible change is a
plan item. Each optimization sits behind a `pc-set-perf-opt` bit (all on by
default), so its saving is measured as an A/B/A marginal (`sandover-perf --toggles opt-bit<N>`)
and its image checked with `python -m sandover.rig.pixdiff` against a capture with the bit off.

## Shared helper and renderer core

Four areas wrote their own GL query caches. deep-001 has one shared helper,
`opengl_renderer/GlQueries.h`: `GLQ_LOC(bit, program, name)` (per-call-site uniform cache),
`glq::uniform_loc`, and `glq::SavedState` (save/restore queried every 256th frame,
`SANDOVER_GL_STATE_CHECK=1` to verify). Each use names its area's bit, so savings stay per area.
Areas port to it before their merge. Open core item (bits 28-31): track the GL state we set in C++
so no pass queries the driver at all (per the tile-lights work, fixing one pass's queries only
moves the driver sync to the next pass); measured by CPU `render` time once the tile-light
readback is gone.

## Morning summary

**What was done.** Every render pass was profiled at 4K MSAA 4, HDR, with every feature on, from
21 spots (baseline 20260928-0614: 6.6-20.5 ms per frame, all GPU-bound). Then six areas were
optimized in parallel. Each change sits behind its own switch bit, so it could be measured on
and off and checked for image parity on a paused frame. Everything that passed is merged on
`perf/deep-001` and on by default. Nothing that changes the look was shipped.

**Result.** Against the baseline, frames are 2.3 ms faster on average (19% shorter; run 1028,
with bit 8 taken as vista-4 measured it in run 1132). By spot:
- 2.2-3.7 ms faster at 13 spots: beach -3.72, snow -3.70, village2 night -3.65, maincave
  -3.39, jungle -3.22, swamp dock -3.09, swamp -3.07, village1 hut lamps -3.03, sunken -2.91,
  darkcave-0025 -2.46, open-sea-high -2.44, darkcave -2.26, maincave crystals -2.22;
- 0.9-2.0 ms faster at 6: temple-top about -2.0 (with vista-4's bit 8), darkcave-0024 -1.90,
  ogre -1.87, low-behind-jak -1.61, open-sea-low -1.19, hut-roof -0.93;
- flat at 2: village1 at night +0.02, finalboss -0.19.

Nine of the 21 are faster beyond their noise band; the rest are faster by about the same amount
but sit at spots with a wide band (beach, the open-sea and hut-roof spots, low-behind-jak).
Switching every optimization off at each spot costs 3.1 ms on average. The biggest single wins:
- tile lights, 1.3 ms, up to 2.3: tiles ranked on the CPU, no mid-frame GPU readback stall,
  cached uniforms;
- SSAO, 0.7: depth read straight from the MSAA buffer;
- water, up to 1.6 at sea: zero-weight shading skipped, paired patches share one envmap pass;
- vista, up to 2 at temple-top: shrub chunk cull, sub-pixel cut, off-screen neighbours skipped,
  nearest-first order where levels cannot tie.

In SDR output, the new flip-model present saves another 1.0-1.5 ms.

**Image.** A paused-frame A/B of the shipping build against everything off, at all 21 spots:
15 identical (max channel difference <= 2), 2 no different than the frame compared with itself
(open-sea-high, village1-night). The other four differ by 3-18 levels on at most 0.025% of the
pixels, none of it visible: hut-roof and low-behind-jak (3-4 levels), maincave (11 levels on
0.004%), snow (falling particles).

**Draw order.** Vista's nearest-first order (bit 8) first changed a clifftop's shading at
open-sea-high: neighbouring levels share coincident geometry at their seams, and the level drawn
later wins the tie. It ships with the order kept between levels that can tie (vista-4), which is
exact and saves 0.77 ms at temple-top.

**Tooling added.** Per-pass GPU sub-scopes and counters, `sandover-perf` sets, custom masks,
compare with noise bands, JSON digests, build lanes held while measuring (concurrent builds
had inflated merc by up to 0.4 ms), paused-frame A/B with a per-shot A/A2 floor, and a prune fix
that stopped the rig deleting hand-staged builds.

## Review notes (2026-09-30)

What the evidence above does and does not cover:

- Bits 16 (direct present) and 19 (MSAA averaged in the final pass) are off on screenshot
  frames (`do_pcrtc_effects` gets `direct_ok = !save_screenshot`), so the paused-frame A/B never
  exercised them. Their evidence is the `SANDOVER_HDR_DIRECT_CHECK` log (the direct image
  compared with the separate encode on check frames), not the A/B.
- Bit 24 (SDR DXGI flip present) has not been tried with exclusive fullscreen, alt-tab,
  overlays or VRR.
- Bit 11's sub-pixel cut sits in `Shrub::make_culled_multidraws`, so it also applies to the main
  levels' shrubs whenever bit 23 (main-level shrub cull) is on, not to vista alone.
- Bit 0 bundles three things: the uniform-location caches, the cached save/restore of the
  emissive-lights pass, and the asynchronous light readback, which lights the frame with the
  previous frame's candidates (one frame of latency).
- The `perf_opt` uniform location (`perf::set_opt_uniform`) is cached under bit 29.
- `make_fbo` always makes the depth-stencil a texture (for bit 4's direct MSAA depth read),
  whatever the mask; the all-off mask therefore does not restore the upstream renderbuffer.

Review fixes on top of dc9991e98:

- `glq::SavedState` copies are now dropped on a state-generation change (targets rebuilt,
  window, size, MSAA, HDR, stereo/XR, letterbox or any GfxGlobalSettings byte; level textures
  and VAOs deleted) and on any perf-mask change, besides the 256-frame refresh.
