# Performance backlog

Costs recorded during the build phases and left alone on purpose: no rendering pass is tuned
until the techniques are settled. Add a row when a cost
is measured or a known inefficiency is shipped; the performance pass works through this list.

| Item | Where | Cost / symptom | Idea | Found |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `materials.csv` read at runtime | `Materials.cpp` (`load_table_locked`) | one parse of a ~4000-row CSV on the loader thread at the first Jak 1 level load, toggle on or off; shipping depends on a data-dir file | bake the class byte into the fr3 or ship a compact index next to the maps | review |
| Per-texture maps: full-resolution RGBA8 | `Materials.cpp` (`upload_level_maps`) | maps are uploaded at the derived size (the 4x pack size, 16x the vanilla albedo texels); see the p5 maps finding for MB per level | BC5 normal + BC4 roughness, or a half-res option (derive or upload at 2x pack size, a quarter of the VRAM; `[maps] max_size` in `presets/pbr.toml` is the runtime cap) | p5 maps engine |
| Per-texture maps: duplicated per level | `Materials.cpp` | a texture drawn by two loaded levels (or a level and the common textures) is decoded and uploaded twice | share uploads by `<tpage>/<name>` with a refcount (also fixes generic falling back to flat when the later of two levels sharing a `combo_id` unloads first) | p5 maps engine |
| Maps decoded on the render thread when the toggle is turned on | `Materials.cpp` (`PbrDrawState::begin`) | levels loaded before the toggle decode their maps synchronously at the first shaded draw (a one-off hitch, logged in ms); levels loaded with the toggle on decode on the loader thread | decode on a worker and bind the flat default until it lands | p5 maps engine |
| `index.json` parsed as a whole | `Materials.cpp` (`map_index`) | ~4000 entries with fields the engine does not read; parsed once, on the first decode | a compact engine index written by `derive-maps` | p5 maps engine |
| Emissive tile lights: tile pass | `emissive_tiles.frag`, `emissive_select.vert` (`EmissiveLights.cpp`) | 1.6 ms total at 2560x1440 MSAA 4 (one solo measurement); the tile pass is the likely hotspot: 2304 fragments each read ~400 taps serially (low occupancy); the select pass ranks each lit tile against up to every other tile | two-level reduction for the tiles, a parallel top-32 for the select | review |
| Ocean tessellation: mid ocean 434k triangles | `ocean_water.tesc` (`water_level`) | mid ocean 1.35k source triangles become ~434k, near 486 become ~21k (`WATER-PRIMS`, beach shot); `ocean-mid` +0.17 ms, all buckets +0.19 ms at 2560x1440 MSAA 4 (one solo run). Most of it is the fixed 0.75 m segments out to 250 m | tessellation level by distance (world segment growing with range, or pixel-based everywhere with a seam-safe rule), cap the inner level, cull patches off screen in the control stage | review |
| Parallax offset on masonry | `pbr_bg_frag.glsl` (`pbr_parallax`, `pbr_parallax_albedo`) | tfrag/TIE/etie draws with a packed map only: up to 3 dependent height taps inside the fade range plus a second albedo fetch on every such fragment, before the alpha test (so discarded texels pay it too); cost not yet measured solo | (taps already skipped at zero fade, review fix) early-out when the first tap is at the top, drop to 1 tap past the fade start, single albedo fetch at zero fade | review |
